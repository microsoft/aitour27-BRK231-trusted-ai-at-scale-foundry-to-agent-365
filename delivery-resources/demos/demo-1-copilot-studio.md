## What this demo shows

Supplier contracts become decision intelligence. A sudden demand surge creates a manufacturing
capacity gap, and Charlotte Waltson, VP of Procurement, needs to identify which contract
manufacturers can support production without compromising quality, compliance, cost, or
delivery.

The **supplier intelligence agent** is grounded in contracts, sourcing policies, and supplier
data. It doesn't just find the contract. It interprets scope, checks exceptions, and shows
exactly which clauses support the recommendation.

1. **Understand** — interprets capabilities and obligations hidden in supplier contracts.
2. **Compare** — evaluates capacity, regulatory, quality, commercial, and delivery terms.
3. **Recommend** — returns an evidence-backed supplier recommendation with clear risks.

## Expected result

**Recommended starting point: Aster Ridge Biomanufacturing.** The existing SOW already covers
the core manufacturing scope for ALLER tablets:

- Tablet compression: covered
- Film coating: covered
- Visual inspection: covered
- Standard bottle packaging: included
- Watch item: blister packs or market-specific cartons still require written authorization

**Outcome:** faster, defensible procurement decisions that help critical medicines reach
patients on time.
